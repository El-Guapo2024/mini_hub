# a/b + b/a Trick

Let's look at when we add the two fractions $\frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{a}$:

$$
\begin{array}{rl}
\dfrac{a}{b} + \dfrac{b}{a} &= \dfrac{a^2 + b^2}{ab} \\[8pt]
&= \dfrac{2ab + (a-b)^2}{ab} \\[8pt]
&= \dfrac{2ab}{ab} + \dfrac{(a-b)^2}{ab} \\[8pt]
&= 2 + \dfrac{(a-b)^2}{ab}
\end{array}
$$

Here is an example:

$$
\frac{5}{7} + \frac{7}{5} = 2 + \frac{(7-5)^2}{7 \cdot 5} = 2\frac{4}{\mathbf{35}}
$$

There are some variations to this trick. For example:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{11}{13} + \frac{2}{11} \\
&= \left(\frac{11}{13} + \frac{13}{11}\right) - \frac{11}{11} \\
&= 2 + \frac{2^2}{143} - 1 \\
&= 1\frac{4}{\mathbf{143}}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is a popular variation that is used especially on the last column of the test because the trick is there but not as obvious.

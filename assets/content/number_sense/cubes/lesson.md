# Cubes (Memorized)

The following cubes should also be memorized:

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
5^3 = 125  & 6^3 = 216   & 7^3 = 343   & 8^3 = 512  \\
9^3 = 729  & 10^3 = 1000 & 11^3 = 1331 & 12^3 = 1728 \\
13^3 = 2197 & 14^3 = 2744 & 15^3 = 3375 & 16^3 = 4096 \\
17^3 = 4913 & 18^3 = 5832 & 19^3 = 6859 & 20^3 = 8000
\end{array}
$$

Again, only FOIL when necessary on the practice problems.

**Sum of cubes identity:** $a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)$. This lets you recognize a factored expression as a sum of cubes without expanding it out. For example, if $x=7$, then $(x+3)(x^2-3x+9)$ matches the pattern with $a=x=7$ and $b=3$, so it equals $x^3+3^3 = 7^3+27 = 343+27 = \mathbf{370}$.

**Estimating a product of three consecutive integers:** for something like $89 \times 90 \times 91$, treat the three numbers as approximately equal to the middle one and cube it: $89\times90\times91 \approx 90^3 = 729000$, which falls inside the allowed estimate range of $692464$ to $765356$, since the exact value is $728910\text{.}$ The same works for $119\times120\times121 \approx 120^3 = 1728000$, inside the range $1641486$ to $1814374\text{,}$ since the exact value is $1727880\text{.}$

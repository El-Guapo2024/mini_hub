# Distance and Velocity Conversions

The following are important conversion factors for distance and velocities:

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1 \text{ mile} = 5280 \text{ ft.} & 1 \text{ ft/hr} = \dfrac{1}{5} \text{ in/min} \\[6pt]
1 \text{ mile} = 1760 \text{ yd.} & 1 \text{ mile/hr} = \dfrac{88}{5} \text{ in/s} \\[6pt]
1 \text{ mile/hr} = \dfrac{22}{15} \text{ ft/s} & 1 \text{ inch} = 2.54 \text{ cm} \\[6pt]
1 \text{ ft/min} = \dfrac{1}{5} \text{ in/s} &
\end{array}
$$

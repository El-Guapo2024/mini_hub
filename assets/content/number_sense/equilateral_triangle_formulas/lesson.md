# Equilateral Triangle Area from Side

For an equilateral triangle with side length $s$:

$$
\text{Area} = \frac{s^2 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{4}
$$

For an equilateral triangle with height $h$:

$$
\text{Area} = \frac{h^2 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{3}
$$

Finding the height when given the side length $s$:

$$
\text{Height} = \frac{s \cdot \sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

**Example:** An equilateral triangle's perimeter is 12. Its area is $4k \cdot \sqrt{3}$. What is $k$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
s \\
&= \frac{12}{3} \\
&= 4 \Rightarrow A \\
&= \frac{4^2 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{4} \\
&= 4\sqrt{3} \Rightarrow k \\
&= \mathbf{1}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Example:** An equilateral triangle has a height of 4, what is its side length?

$$
\begin{aligned}
h \\
&= 4 \\
&= \frac{\sqrt{3} \cdot s}{2} \Rightarrow s \\
&= \frac{4 \cdot 2}{\sqrt{3}} \\
&= \mathbf{\frac{8\sqrt{3}}{3}}
\end{aligned}
$$

# Multiplying by 101 Trick

In the same spirit as the multiplying by 11's trick, multiplying by 101 involves adding gap connected digits. Let's look at an example:

$$
438 \times 101 = \begin{array}{ll}
\text{Ones:}        & 1 \times 8 & \mathbf{8} \\
\text{Tens:}        & 1 \times 3 & \mathbf{3} \\
\text{Hundreds:}    & 1 \times 4 + 1 \times 8 & {\color{gray}1}\mathbf{2} \\
\text{Thousands:}   & 1 \times 3 + 1 & \mathbf{4} \\
\text{Tens Thousands:} & 1 \times 4 & \mathbf{4} \\
\text{Answer:}      & \mathbf{44238}
\end{array}
$$

So you see, immediately you can write down the ones/tens digits (they are the same as what you are multiplying 101 with). Then you sum gap digits and move down the line. Let's look at another example:

$$
8234 \times 101 = \begin{array}{ll}
\text{Ones/Tens:}        & \mathbf{34} & \mathbf{34} \\
\text{Hundreds:}         & 2 + 4 & \mathbf{6} \\
\text{Thousands:}        & 8 + 3 & {\color{gray}1}\mathbf{1} \\
\text{Tens Thousands:}   & 2 + 1 & \mathbf{3} \\
\text{Hundred Thousands:}& 8 & \mathbf{8} \\
\text{Answer:}           & \mathbf{831634}
\end{array}
$$

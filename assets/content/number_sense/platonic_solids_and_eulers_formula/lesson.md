# Platonic Solids and Euler's Formula

The following is a list of important characteristics of Platonic Solids which are popularly asked on the test:

$$
\begin{array}{lllll}
\textbf{Platonic Solid} & \textbf{Face Polygons} & \textbf{\# of Faces} & \textbf{\# of Vertices} & \textbf{\# of Edges} \\
\hline
\text{Tetrahedron}  & \text{Triangles} & 4  & 4  & 6  \\
\text{Cube}         & \text{Squares}   & 6  & 8  & 12 \\
\text{Octahedron}   & \text{Triangles} & 8  & 6  & 12 \\
\text{Dodecahedron} & \text{Pentagons} & 12 & 20 & 30 \\
\text{Icosahedron}  & \text{Triangles} & 20 & 12 & 30 \\
\end{array}
$$

If you ever forget one of the characteristics of the solids but remember the other two, you can always use Euler's formula:

$$
\text{Faces} + \text{Vertices} - \text{Edges} = 2
$$

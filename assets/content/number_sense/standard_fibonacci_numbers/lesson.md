# Standard Fibonacci Numbers

It's becoming crucial to have at least the first fourteen Fibonacci numbers memorized as the test writers tend to ask at least two questions on each test that stem from directly knowing them. It will also help immensely with reducing the calculations required in the Sum of the First n Fibonacci Numbers topic and the Fibonacci sequence topics that follow. Below is a table of the first fourteen Fibonacci numbers:

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
F_1 = 1   & F_2 = 1   & F_3 = 2   & F_4 = 3   \\
F_5 = 5   & F_6 = 8   & F_7 = 13  & F_8 = 21  \\
F_9 = 34  & F_{10} = 55 & F_{11} = 89 & F_{12} = 144 \\
F_{13} = 233 & F_{14} = 377 & &
\end{array}
$$

# Sum of Consecutive Squares

Usually when approached with this problem, one of the squares ends in 5 making the squaring of the number relatively trivial. You want to use the approach of factoring to help aid in these problems. For example:

$$
\begin{aligned}
35^2 + 36^2 \\
&= 35^2 + (35+1)^2 \\
&= 2 \cdot 35^2 + 2 \cdot 35 + 1^2 \\
&= 2 \cdot 1225 + 70 + 1 \\
&= \mathbf{2521}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is a brute force technique, however, it is a lot better than squaring both of the numbers and then adding them together (which you can get lost very easily doing that).

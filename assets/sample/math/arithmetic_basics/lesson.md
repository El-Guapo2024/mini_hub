# Arithmetic Basics

Addition combines two numbers into a sum: $a + b = c$

Subtraction finds the difference between two numbers: $a - b = c$

Multiplication and fractions look like this: $\frac{a}{b} \times c = \frac{ac}{b}$

Exponents and roots: $x^2 + \sqrt{y} = z$

Sums and limits: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ and $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$

Integrals: $\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2} \, dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$

Matrices: $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} ax+by \\ cx+dy \end{pmatrix}$

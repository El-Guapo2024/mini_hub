# Multiplying by 25 Trick

The trick to multiplying by 25 is to think of it as $\frac{100}{4}$. So the strategy is to take whatever you are multiplying with, divide it by 4 then move the decimal over to the right two places. Here are a couple of examples:

$$
84 \times 25 = \frac{84}{4} \times 100 = 21 \times 100 = \mathbf{2100}
$$

$$
166 \times 25 = \frac{166}{4} \times 100 = 41.5 \times 100 = \mathbf{4150}
$$

In a similar manner, you can use the same principle to divide numbers by 25 easily. The difference is you multiply by 4 and then move the decimal over to the left two places:

$$
\frac{415}{25} = \frac{415}{\frac{100}{4}} = \frac{415 \times 4}{100} = \frac{1660}{100} = 16.6
$$

# Special Integers

The following integers have important properties which are exploited regularly on the number sense test. They are:

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathbf{999}: & 999 = 27 \times 37 \\
\mathbf{1073}: & 1073 = 29 \times 37 \\
\mathbf{77}: & 77 = \dfrac{1001}{13} \\[8pt]
\mathbf{154}: & 154 = \dfrac{2002}{13} \\[8pt]
\vdots & \\
\mathbf{693}: & 693 = \dfrac{9009}{13} \\[8pt]
\mathbf{3367}: & 3367 = \dfrac{10101}{3} \\[8pt]
\mathbf{1443}: & 1443 = \dfrac{10101}{7} \\[8pt]
\mathbf{1430}: & 1430 = \dfrac{10010}{7}
\end{array}
$$

The following are some examples showing how to use these special numbers:

**999 Trick:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
333 \times \frac{1}{27} \times \frac{1}{37} \\
&= \frac{1}{3} \times \mathbf{999} \times \frac{1}{27} \times \frac{1}{37}
\end{aligned}
= \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{27 \cdot 37}{27 \cdot 37}
= \mathbf{\frac{1}{3}}
$$

**1001 Trick:**

$$
385 \times 13 = 77 \times 5 \times 13
= \frac{\mathbf{1001}}{13} \times 5 \times 13
= 1001 \times 5
= \mathbf{5005}
$$

**10101 Trick:**

$$
1443 \times 56 = \frac{\mathbf{10101}}{7} \times 56
= 10101 \times \frac{56}{7}
= 10101 \times 8
= \mathbf{80808}
$$

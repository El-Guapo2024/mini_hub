# π and e Approximations

Using the standard approximations $\pi \approx 3.1$, $e \approx 2.7$, and $e^2 \approx 7.4$ lead to the beneficial results of:

$$
\pi^2 \approx 10, \quad e^3 \approx 20, \quad \pi \cdot e \approx 8.5
$$

Knowing these values, we can approximate various powers of $e$ and $\pi$ relatively simple and within the required margin of error of $\pm 5\%$. The following is an example where these approximations are useful:

$$
(e \times \pi)^4 = e^4 \times \pi^4
= e \cdot e^3 \cdot (\pi^2)^2
\approx e \cdot 20 \cdot 100
\approx e \cdot 2000
\approx \mathbf{5400}
$$

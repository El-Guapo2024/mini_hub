# Cube Volume and Surface Area

Usually basic formulas for spheres, cubes, cones, and cylinders are fair game for the Number Sense test. In order to solve these problems, memorize the following table (where $s$ is the side-length, $r$ is the radius, $h$ is the height, and $l$ is the slant height):

$$
\begin{array}{lll}
\textbf{Type of Solid} & \textbf{Volume} & \textbf{Surface Area} \\[4pt]
\text{Cube}     & s^3                   & 6s^2 \\[4pt]
\text{Sphere}   & \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3   & 4\pi r^2 \\[8pt]
\text{Cone}     & \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h & \pi r l + \pi r^2 \\[8pt]
\text{Cylinder} & \pi r^2 h             & 2\pi r h
\end{array}
$$

In addition to knowing the above formulas, a couple of other ones are:

- Face Diagonal of a Cube $= s\sqrt{2}$
- Body Diagonal of a Cube $= s\sqrt{3}$

# Sum of Squares of Arbitrary Fibonacci Sequence

For the sum of the squares of the first $n$ terms of an Arbitrary Fibonacci Sequence $(A_1^2 + A_2^2 + A_3^2 + \ldots + A_n^2)$, the formula is:

$$
A_1^2 + A_2^2 + \ldots + A_n^2 = A_n \times A_{n+1} - A_1(A_2 - A_1)
$$

Notice that if a Standard Fibonacci Sequence is used, the last term cancels because $F_2 - F_1 = 0$, so the sum is simply $F_n \times F_{n+1}$. A quick example is:

$$
1^2 + 1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + \ldots + 21^2 + 34^2 = 34 \times 55 = \mathbf{1870}
$$

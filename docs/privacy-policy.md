# TheMiniHub — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

TheMiniHub does not collect, store or transmit any personal information to its
developer. There is no account, no analytics, and no server operated by this
app.

Everything the app records — the books you open, where you are in them, the
flashcards you save and your practice history — is stored only on your own
device.

## Services you can connect

The app can connect to three outside services. Each one you set up yourself
with your own credentials, and none of them is used unless you do:

- **Anthropic (Claude)** — if you add a Claude API key, the passage on screen,
  and the words you select or the question you ask about it, are sent to
  Anthropic to answer.
- **Microsoft Azure Speech** — if you add an Azure Speech key, the companion's
  answer text is sent to Microsoft to be spoken aloud.
- **AnkiWeb** — if you connect an Anki account, the flashcards you save are
  synced to AnkiWeb.

Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies.

With none of them configured, the app makes no network requests at all: the
reader, the dictionary, the card store and reading aloud all work on the
device.

## Your keys

Your API keys and your AnkiWeb sync key are held in your phone's secure
storage — the iOS Keychain, or the Android Keystore — and are never sent
anywhere except to the service they belong to. Your AnkiWeb password is never
stored.

## Speech

Speech recognition runs entirely on your device. Audio is never uploaded.

## Backups

If your phone backs up its apps (iCloud, or Android's backup to your Google
account), that backup includes what this app stores on the device. It is made
under your own account and never reaches the developer. On Android, your keys
are left out of it.

## Removing your data

Removing a connector on the Connectors screen deletes its credentials from
your device. Deleting the app removes everything else.
